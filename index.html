One app for the whole school

Teachers, parents and school owners stay connected — announcements, homework, attendance, exams, report cards, surveys and more, in English, हिन्दी and தமிழ்.

📝

Homework & Study Material

Post homework with images and PDFs, save private drafts to publish later, filter by class, and see who has read it.

✅

Attendance & Late Marking

Quick daily attendance with late-hours tracking, leave management, monthly summaries and working-hours insights.

🏖️

Leave Management

Students and staff apply for leave in-app — approvals, status tracking, reject reasons and leave summaries, all in one place.

📊

Exams & Report Cards

Create exams, per-subject dates and portions, enter marks with absent support, and auto-generate report cards with grades.

📢

Notices & Announcements

Reach the whole school or specific classes; include images, PDFs and videos that stay fresh and organised.

🎥

Photo & Video Sharing

Share event photos and videos with parents through notices and homework — auto-removed after a few days to stay tidy.

🔔

Push Notifications

Every announcement, homework, result or leave update reaches parents and staff instantly on their phone.

🗂️

Owners, Admins & Teachers

Role-based access — approvals, employees, branches, monthly themes, cafeteria and more for the management team.

🏢

Franchise & Branch Management

Run multiple school branches and franchises from one control centre — each with its own staff, classes, data and dashboard.

🏆

Hall of Fame

Celebrate student achievements with a dedicated Hall of Fame — winners' moments shared with families and the school community.

💬

Parent Messages

A two-way inbox between parents and the school for quick, organised communication.

📋

Parent Surveys

A curated multilingual question bank so parents can share feedback on every exam — reviewed privately by staff.

📅

Yearly Calendar & Schedules

An academic-year calendar with events, celebrations, monthly themes, traditional games and diet charts — shared with every family.

👁️

Read Receipts

Teachers and owners see exactly who has read each homework and announcement — one glance at who is up to date.

🌐

Multilingual

English, हिन्दी and தமிழ் out of the box, with a per-user language preference that follows each person.

White-label

It becomes your school's own app

We replace the branding with yours — your logo, your name, your colours. Your school looks like the developer, while we build, host and support.

🚀 Your brand, everywhere

Your school emblem, app name and colour scheme across the app, the Play Store listing and every notification.

📱 Android + Web

Runs on Android phones and in the browser — parents and teachers can use whichever they prefer.

🗃️ Private, isolated data

Each school gets its own secure database. Your school's families, children and records stay separate and private.

🧑‍🏫 Setup & support included

We import your students, classes and staff, train your team, and stay on call for ongoing support.

🔒 Privacy-first

Data is encrypted in transit, access is role-controlled, and every school provides its own privacy policy to families.

📈 Grows with you

Multi-branch support is built in — add branches or franchises as your school expands.
